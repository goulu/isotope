# Levels Guide — ISOThOPE

Detailed descriptions, historical background, and nuclear strategies for all 23 levels of **ISOThOPE**.

---

## Tutorial 1: Electron Capture

- **Mission:** `Te-123` ➔ `Sb-123`
- **Resources:** 1 ⚡ electron(s)

The Tellurium-123 atom (Te-123) is a special case of a stable isobar. Add an electron (Right Click or Down Arrow) to transmute a proton into a neutron. You will instantly obtain stable Antimony!

## Tutorial 2: Beta- Radioactivity

- **Mission:** `O-18` ➔ `F-19`
- **Resources:** 1 ⚛ neutron(s)

Oxygen-18 is stable. Add a neutron (Left Click or Right Arrow) to turn it into Oxygen-19. It will become unstable and spontaneously decay (Beta-) into Fluorine-19!

---

## Level 3: The Alchemist's Dream

- **Mission:** `Hg-196` ➔ `Au-197`
- **Resources:** 1 ⚛ neutron(s) • 1 ⚡ electron(s)

Transmutation of Mercury-196 into stable Gold. This reaction was achieved in 1941 by Sherr, Bainbridge, and Anderson by bombarding mercury with neutrons.

## Level 4: The Radioactive Cascade

- **Mission:** `Pb-208` ➔ `Tl-205`
- **Resources:** 1 ⚛ neutron(s)

Lead-208 is nature's heaviest stable nuclide. Add a neutron (⚛) to turn it into unstable Lead-209. It will first undergo Beta- decay (to Bismuth-209), followed by Alpha decay to stable Thallium-205!

## Level 5: Reactive Calcium

- **Mission:** `Ca-46` ➔ `Ti-48`
- **Resources:** 2 ⚛ neutron(s)

Calcium-46 is stable. Add a neutron (⚛) to form Calcium-47, and wait for it to decay into unstable Scandium-47. As soon as it appears, quickly fire a second neutron (⚛) before it decays to get Scandium-48, which will stabilize into Titanium-48!

## Level 12: Lead Juggling

- **Mission:** `Pb-208` ➔ `Tl-205`
- **Resources:** 1 ⚛ neutron(s) • 3 ⚡ electron(s)

Lead-208 is stable. Add a neutron (⚛) to get unstable Lead-209, which decays into Bismuth-209. As soon as Bismuth appears, quickly shoot an electron (⚡) to turn it back into Lead-209. Repeat this juggling 3 times to use up all your electrons, then let the cascade stabilize into Thallium-205!

## Level 13: The Great Neon Race

- **Mission:** `Ne-22` ➔ `Mg-24`
- **Resources:** 3 ⚛ neutron(s) • 2 ⚡ electron(s)

Neon-22 is stable. Add a neutron (⚛), then very rapidly fire two more neutrons (⚛, ⚛) and two electrons (⚡, ⚡) without waiting for intermediate isotopes to decay. You will reach unstable Oxygen-25, which will undergo a spectacular decay cascade all the way to stable Magnesium-24!

## Level 14: The Alpha Escape

- **Mission:** `Sm-144` ➔ `Pr-141`
- **Resources:** 1 ⚛ neutron(s) • 1 ⚡ electron(s)

Samarium-144 is stable. Particle order is critical here: first fire an electron (⚡) to produce Promethium-144, which will undergo Beta+ decay to Neodymium-144, followed by an Alpha decay (🟨) to Cerium-140! Wait for it to stabilize, then add a neutron (⚛): Cerium-141 will Beta- decay into stable Praseodymium-141. Firing the neutron first would have trapped you forever in the Neodymium-145 valley!

## Level 15: The Proton Reflex

- **Mission:** `He-4` ➔ `Li-6`
- **Resources:** 1 ⚛ neutron(s) • 1 🔺 proton(s)

Helium-4 is extraordinarily stable. Fire a proton (🔺/Key P) to form Lithium-5, or a neutron (⚛) to form Helium-5. Beware: both isotopes are ultra-fast spontaneous emitters that will immediately spit the particle back out if you hesitate! Fire the second particle in a fraction of a second to stabilize the nucleus into Lithium-6!

## Level 16: Cerium Interception

- **Mission:** `Sm-144` ➔ `Ce-142`
- **Resources:** 2 ⚛ neutron(s) • 1 ⚡ electron(s)

Samarium-144 must transmute into Cerium-142. Fire an electron (⚡), let the Beta+ and Alpha cascade descend to Cerium-140. Fire a first neutron (⚛) to form Cerium-141, and immediately intercept it with a second neutron (⚛) before it has time to decay into Praseodymium-141!

## Level 17: The Quantum Shortcut

- **Mission:** `Li-7` ➔ `He-3`
- **Resources:** 1 ⚡ electron(s) • 1 🔺 proton(s)

Lithium-7 can reach Helium-3 through a spectacular nuclear pathway. Inject a proton (🔺) to form Beryllium-8, which undergoes instant Alpha decay into Helium-4. Then capture an electron (⚡) to reach Hydrogen-4: it will spontaneously emit a neutron to form Tritium (H-3), decaying Beta- into stable Helium-3!

## Level 18: Neodymium Valley

- **Mission:** `Nd-143` ➔ `Ce-142`
- **Resources:** 2 ⚛ neutron(s) • 1 🔺 proton(s)

Neodymium-143 is surrounded by isobaric traps. Adding neutrons first will plunge you irreversibly into stable Neodymium-146. You must fire the proton (🔺) first to pass through Promethium-144, await Alpha decay from Neodymium-144, then quickly chain two neutrons (⚛, ⚛) on Cerium!

## Level 19: Thallium Pipe Organ

Thallium-205 must be converted into Lead-204. First add a neutron (⚛) to form Thallium-206 and let it transmute into Lead-206. Then inject two consecutive protons (🔺, 🔺) to climb through Bismuth-207 into Polonium-208, which will eject an Alpha particle directly into stable Lead-204!

Tungsten-182 is a metallic fortress. Fire two consecutive protons (🔺, 🔺) to create unstable Osmium-184. Watch nature trigger a back-to-back double Alpha decay cascade (Os-184 ➔ W-180 ➔ Hf-176)! Once stabilized in Hafnium-176, add the final neutron (⚛) to reach Hafnium-177.

## Level 23: The Ultimate Alchemy

- **Mission:** `Re-185` ➔ `Hf-180`

Neutron first, wait through the Osmium ➔ Tungsten cascade, inject both protons toward Osmium-184, let the Alpha cascade descend onto Tungsten-180, and finally capture the electron to stabilize into Hafnium-180!
